# Settings

Code: `src/panels/settings.rs`, `src/panels/settings/`, `src/dialogs/pairing.rs`, `src/dialogs/pairing/`, `src/ui/qr.rs`, `src/ui/qr/`

- **Where the code is**: `settings.rs` holds the sheet's state, loading, its fields and the search field; `settings/changes.rs` the commands that change things and the questions asked first; `settings/pages.rs` the navigation, a page, its sections and setting rows; `settings/controls.rs` each setting type's control and the text fields; `settings/choice.rs` the dropdown, a field's suggestions and the popover both open (their pieces in `ui/menu.rs`); `settings/agents.rs` the table of how each agent starts; `settings/sources.rs` the sources checklist; `settings/connection.rs` tokens, bots, paired browsers and what offers pairing them, the forum's lines and its server rows; `settings/forum.rs` the Discord forum's state in the app feed; `settings/projects.rs` the Projects page; `settings/links.rs` the Links page, the window's own settings; `settings/appearance.rs` and `settings/appearance/` the Appearance page (`design/theme.md`); `settings/share.rs` the Share page and `settings/import.rs` importing a shared setup and its preview; `settings/search.rs` the search results; `settings/data.rs` what `config --json` answers, how it is grouped and searched, tested without a window; `settings/widgets.rs` the pieces of its pages (navigation, headings, cards of rows, the slot, a switch, the dropdown). Chips, the status dot, alert lines, callouts, row actions and a field's small buttons are `ui/signs.rs`, shared with the Accounts sheet.
- **The form is built from `config --json`**: it describes pages, sections, each setting's type, choices and note, so a new cornercase setting shows up without a change here.
- **Each change is saved at once** (`config set`, `config secret`, `config remove`, `config project`, all with `--json`), and the answer, which carries the new revision, replaces what the sheet shows. Notices in the answer become a toast. One change per row runs at a time: a second press while it runs is ignored.
- **The sheet reads the settings again** when it opens and when the session's `config.revision` grows, which tells that they changed elsewhere (the TUI, an edit of `config.json`). A revision only compares within one server boot, so a new boot reloads too. A failed read says why, with "Try again".

## The layout

Asked for on 2026-10-31 ("improve the whole Settings section, the modal, the UI and the UX of every page"). The sheet was one generic form: switching page kept the last page's scroll, so a page could open halfway down; a text field moved left when its Save and Revert buttons appeared beside it, and a row's control moved when its reset glyph appeared; long choices opened as a list that pushed the rows below down; a token's state was a small grey word beside its label; the sources were chips with `‹ ›` arrows and invisible arrows taking room; a project's long folder ran out of its card; nothing helped find one of seventy settings. Now, in the look the Accounts sheet took the same day (`design/accounts.md`):

- **Navigation**: a search field on top, focused when the sheet opens; the pages with their icons (the panels' own where one exists: Worktrees, Issues, the agent; an app window for UI, a phone and screen for Remote), cornercase's pages first, then Projects and Share after a line; a red dot on a page with a refused token; at the foot the file the settings live in, home as `~`, ending in "…" when it does not fit and whole in its tooltip.
- **A page** is a column at most 800 px wide, centred: its icon, title and what it holds, then its sections, each an uppercase caption over a card of rows. A section of one row named as the section (Sources shown) drops its caption. Changing page, or searching, scrolls back to the top.
- **A row** is the label and its note, the control on the right, then a fixed slot: the reset arrow (`Back to the default: 5 min`) once the value differs from cornercase's default, a spinner while a change runs, or nothing. The slot keeps its width, so no control moves when its reset appears. A setting that needs a restart (`restart`) says so in an orange chip. When the label and the control do not fit side by side (a 300 px field in a narrow window), the control wraps under the label (the row wraps, the label's basis is 230 px) rather than squeezing the note into a column of a few words; switches and numbers stay beside it.
- **Text fields keep their place**: Save (✓) and Revert (✕) show inside the field, at its end, as soon as it differs from the saved value, so the field never moves while typing. Enter saves; Escape reverts an edited field, and when nothing is edited it goes on to close the sheet (`TextField::reverts_on_escape`, `design/widgets.md`). After a save a green check shows in the field for 1.6 s. While a field is focused, cornercase's own prompt for it (`field`, such as the placeholders `{key} {number} {slug}` of issue branches) shows under the row, when it says more than the label and the note. A number shows the unit cornercase writes after it (`5 min`), read from `shown`; at 0, where `shown` says `off`, there is none. Every field is a tab stop.
- **Choices**: a few short ones (up to four, 18 characters, none dangerous) are segments, each with its note as a tooltip; the others are a dropdown that opens a popover under it with cornercase's question, a check on the value in use, each value's note when it says more than the value, and dangerous ones in red with an alert. ↑ and ↓ move through it, Enter chooses, Escape or a click outside closes it (only it: the sheet stays). The mouse and the arrows share one highlight, kept in the sheet's state, because gpui 0.2.2 cannot recolour text on hover (`design/widgets.md`). A dropdown whose value in use is dangerous is red too. Values read with spaces for underscores (`projects on top`).
- **A field with suggestions** (#712 upstream, cornercase 0.13.21): a setting edited as text that has `choices` (only `shell` today; cornercase says they are suggestions, not a limit) stays the 300 px text field, with free entry, Save and Revert inside it, and a chevron button at its end after them, so the field never moves. The chevron, or ↓ in the focused field (a hook of `TextField::on_down`, so it does not depend on the order of key listeners and actions), opens the dropdown's own popover under the field; a pick fills the field, focuses it and saves at once. ↑ and ↓ move, Enter picks, Escape closes only the popover and gives the field its focus back, so a second Escape reverts or closes the sheet. A click outside closes the popover without giving the field its focus back, so as not to take it from the field that click lands on; the next Escape then closes the sheet. The popover lists `default` first (the setting's default value, `""`, no note: `config --json` names the default shell only while it is in use, which the empty field's placeholder already shows), then cornercase's suggestions in its order, each value as written (a path keeps its `_`), with a check on the saved one. There is no `another program…` entry, since the field is always there. `default` sends `""`, as the reset arrow does, so re-picking it while in use changes nothing; typing the word `default` clears the arguments too, which is cornercase's rule. Rejected: segments (long paths), a plain field (the shells found would stay unseen), a dropdown with `another program…` opening a field (two controls where one does). The reset arrow of such a field with an empty default says `Back to the default shell`.
- **A warning row**: a setting cornercase marks `dangerous` shows its note in red with the alert icon, and its field's edge is red while it is neither focused nor open (`shell` when the chosen program cannot start, `issue_branch` when GitLab will not close issues from the template, `prefix_key` when it clashes), as the TUI paints the value red. The navigation's red dot stays for refused tokens only, since a dangerous agent mode in use would dot the Agents page for good. Limit: `config.revision` does not move when a chosen shell becomes unstartable, so an open sheet learns it only when it reads the settings again.
- **How each agent starts** is a table: the agent, its mode (a dropdown, or "—" with a tooltip when it has none) and its extra arguments in the terminal's font, under one header instead of a placeholder repeating the agent's name in every field. When the mode is not the default, its arguments show under the agent's name, in red when dangerous. The name and the extra arguments shrink together from their bases (120 and 230 px, the arguments to no less than 120) while the mode keeps 150, and the header has the same sizes, so it stays over its columns in a narrow window.
- **Sources shown** lists each source as the TUI does: the shown ones in their order, each with a tick and arrows to move it, then the hidden ones, unticked. The row says how many are shown.
- **Tokens, bots and browsers** show their state as a dot and words (green connected, with the account; red refused; a ring when not set; `from $VAR` when the environment gives it). A token's field has a key icon and the Connect (or Replace) button beside it from the start, resting until something is typed, then checking while cornercase checks it over the network. Removing a token and forgetting the paired browsers are a trash icon in the row's slot.
- **Asking first goes through the window's dialog** (`dialogs::confirm`, AGENTS.md): choosing a dangerous mode (it names the arguments it adds), removing a token, forgetting every paired browser and clearing a script of several lines. They used to be a red question that opened under the row.
- **Projects**: a card per project, headed by its name (ending in "…" when it does not fit, the whole folder in its tooltip, as the Files panel's header; as a bare text a long one ran past the card; found in #144's sweep), its folder (`~`, ending in "…") and chips (`not open`, `not a git repository`, with what that means), then its setup and teardown in the terminal's font, and for a repository its build folders (`config --json`'s `builds`, saved with `config project --builds`, cornercase 0.12.222): one line separated by spaces, so typing `default` brings the usual list back and an empty field means none, the words of the command; the answer's list replaces what was typed. `config --json` does not say what the usual list is, so there is no reset arrow here: the row's note says what to type. With no project the page says how to get one.
- **A project's menu opens this page on it** (**Project settings…**, as the TUI's project settings, 0.12.108, through `Store::open_on_project`): the page shows that project's card first, the others after it in their order, until the sheet opens again some other way. Rejected: scrolling to its card as a found setting lands (`landing.rs`), since a card that comes first needs no measuring and no mark, and showing only that card, which would need a way back to the others.
- **Share**: Export and Import cards. Export copies the setup or saves it to a file (icon buttons), then says what the last export held and what it left out. Import shows the file, how many settings would change, and each section's changes as the label, the old value faint, an arrow and the new value.

## The window's own settings

- **The sheet's own pages are Appearance, Links, Projects and Share** (Appearance first, below the line that sets them apart). Appearance is the window's theme (`design/theme.md`); it works without the server, is found by its words in both searches, and is not in Export and Import: a theme is a file the person shares by itself.
- **Links is a page of this app's own settings**, kept on this Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/cornercase-gpui/settings.json` (`prefs.rs`; `CORNERCASE_GPUI_SETTINGS` moves it): where a web address opens and which pages its card previews (`design/terminal.md`). They are about this window's Browser panel, which cornercase's TUI does not have, so they are not cornercase's to keep, and they stay here when the window shows another machine's server. Asked for on 2026-11-01.
- **They are drawn as cornercase's settings are**: built as the same `Setting` rows (keys `app.open_links` and `app.preview_pages`, page Links) and added after `config --json` answers, so the segments, notes, the reset arrow and search work unchanged; `save` sends a key of the window's own to `Store::set_pref`, which writes the file at once (a small file, written beside and renamed over), instead of `config set`. The page sits after the line with the sheet's own pages, and the foot of the navigation names that file while it shows. A value this version does not know reads as the default.
- They are not in Export and Import, which are cornercase's (`config export`). Another window reads the file when it starts.
- **The Links page works without the server**: when `config --json` fails, the other pages say so, but Links is drawn from the window's own settings alone (`links::alone`), so previews can still be turned off.

## Search

- **Search covers every setting of cornercase's pages**: a label, note, key, section, cornercase's prompt for the field and the choices' values, ignoring case; every word must match (`claude extra`). The results are the settings' own rows, under `Page › Section`, so a setting changes from there. While searching, each page in the navigation shows how many of its settings match, and the pages with none dim.
- **It is not `config search`, on purpose, and finds what that finds among these rows.** Compared on 2026-11-06 with cornercase 0.13.1, on 74 queries. `config search` takes each word as the start of a word (`ose` finds nothing, `ctrl+b` is two words) over the label, section, page, the value shown and the note; it also lists rows not shown yet and the Accounts, Flows, Plugins and Machines tabs' rows. This search took each word anywhere over the label, note, key, section, prompt and choices. So `hidden`, `never`, `5 min` and `ui sound` found a row in the global search and not here, the same words a person would try in both: it now also looks in the value shown (without a switch's box, so `x` does not find every switch that is on), a connection's state and the page. It stays wider where that helps, on rows `config search` does not find: `ound`, a key (`fetch_minutes`, which the page's own hint offers), a choice not in use (`top` finds the tabs row), `claude extra`. It stays narrower only where this sheet has no such row or no such words: rows `config --json` does not describe yet (`discord_forum`), the other sheets' rows, and what only cornercase's window says of a row (`not connected`, where this sheet says `Not set`; a hidden row's `shows once…`). Rejected: answering from `config search`, a command at each pause for rows drawn here at once; it would lose those finds and could not know the window's own Links rows.
- **Pages match by their name or what they hold** (`teardown` finds Projects, `export` Share): such a page shows as a link when none of its settings is listed already. Clicking a page clears the search. Escape clears it, and then closes the sheet.

## Landing on a row

- **Search's settings rows open the sheet on that row** (`settings/landing.rs`), with nothing changed, as the TUI's `land_on`: `Store::land_on` keeps the answer's row for the sheet to take, whether it is already built or still being built, and the field and the page's scroll start afresh. The focus stays in the search field, so a stray key cannot edit the row.
- **Which row is looked up on every frame**, by the `key` of the answer: a reload can move rows, and the sheet's answer may come after the landing. An agent's `agents.<kind>.mode` or `.extra` is that agent's row of the table, and each of `issue_tabs`' sources is the sources' row. A row without a key, or whose key the sheet does not list, takes the first of its section (`+ another agent…` lands on the agents' table). With no section nothing is marked (`start the tour`, `reset tour progress`, which this sheet does not draw) and a line under the page's heading says the row is in cornercase's own window; a tab Settings has no page for says the same on the page already shown. A tab of a page opens it with nothing marked.
- **`hidden` is about cornercase's own window**: `config --json` still lists `telegram.chat`, `discord.chat` and `browsers` with nothing to pair, and this sheet draws them, so they land on their own row with no notice, and pairing from there says what it needs. `discord_forum` and `discord_forum_close` are not listed until they show: they land on the first row of their section, Discord's bot, with `Forum mode shows once a phone is paired` under it, and nothing is saved, as upstream refuses to reveal such a row by changing state. The notice stays until the search field or another page is used.
- **The mark** is a cobalt tint behind the row, a little wider than it, held for half of 2.6 s and then faded (`motion::mark`). It is one animation that ends, so nothing redraws once it is over and no timer has to clear it.
- **Scrolling measures the row**: gpui 0.2.2's `scroll_to_item` only knows the scrolled element's direct children, and a row sits inside a card inside the column. An empty `canvas` over the row records its bounds while it is laid out and asks for one more frame, which sets the offset, and gpui clamps it to what the page can scroll. A row already wholly in view keeps the page where it is; another comes to a third of the way down, so what is above it still shows.

## Values and tokens

- **A field remembers the saved value it was filled with**: it counts as edited while its text differs, a reload never overwrites an edited field, and after a save the saved value replaces what was typed.
- **An empty value reads as what it means** (`per tracker`, `Plane Cloud`), as the TUI shows it, through the field's placeholder, which follows every answer (a field created holding a value still reads `default (zsh)` once emptied); an empty value cornercase gives no words for reads `none`.
- **`shell.args` is a plain field** sent after `--`, so `-l` is a value; words with a space or `\` go in single quotes, which cornercase's prompt under the row says. Changing the shell clears the arguments (cornercase's rule) and the field follows the answer unless it holds an unsaved edit.
- **Tokens are never shown**: their field is masked (what it shows and why nothing can be copied: `design/widgets.md`), never pre-filled, and cleared as soon as the token is sent on standard input to `config secret KEY`. A token taken from an environment variable wins over a saved one, so it cannot be typed here; its status names the variable.
- **`config set` passes `--` before the key**, so values that start with `-` (an agent's extra arguments) are not read as options. `config project` names an open project by id and any other by folder.
- **Whole numbers are checked before sending**, to say what is wrong under the field rather than as cornercase's error; errors lose the `cornercase config: error:` around them (`cli::reason`) and show under their row with an alert.
- **Pages follow the TUI's tabs** (Worktrees, Agents, Issues, UI, Remote); pages it does not know go after them, then the sheet's own Projects and Share. Sections keep the order they first appear in, connections first (the TUI lists the trackers' accounts before the sources), each connection after its section's settings, untitled ones opening the page. The UI page has no sections in `config --json` (the TUI lists it as one list too), so it is one card here; grouping it would be cornercase's call (`docs/upstream-ideas.md`).
- **The TUI's notes about keys** (`enter pastes one`) do not apply here and are dropped (`Connection::note_here`): a chat's `enter shows a code to scan with your phone` keeps what it shows, and `agents message it while you are away · enter pairs another` loses its second half, which a button says.
- **A script of several lines** does not fit a one-line field: it is shown, and can only be cleared.

## Pairing a phone

- **Each row offers it** (#669, #670 upstream, cornercase 0.13.17): `Pair a browser…` or `Pair another…` on the paired browsers, `Pair…` or `Pair another…` on a chat's, whatever the state, with the chat's name in the row's status (`Connected as Ana`, `account` in `config --json`). `config --json` lists these rows even with nothing to pair, so the button can open on a refusal: asking is how the window learns why not, and greying it out would need the server's rules copied here.
- **A dialog runs one `config pair KEY --json` as a stream beside the queue** (`dialogs/pairing.rs`, `store::follow_until`), since it waits up to ten minutes. The window has no pairing state of its own: the code lives on the server, and the stream is what holds it. It works through `--remote` as any stream does.
- **The stream ends when the dialog closes**, by any way out (Done, Enter, Escape, another dialog, switching servers). The dialog watches `Store::dialog` and drops its `Stream` as soon as it is no longer the dialog, rather than when its entity goes: `Root` keeps a closing dialog for its slide out, and a code must not outlive what shows it. The stream's end after that is ignored, so the slide out does not flash a refusal.
- **What shows is the TUI's**: its titles (`Remote`, `Telegram`, `Discord`), the code, the address under it, the lines the command sends, capitalised, each new `{"url", "lines"}` replacing the last (Discord's advice and failures come this way). `url: null` is a line with a spinner (`Starting Tailscale…`). Added to the TUI's: `Waiting for your phone…`, since a screen that waits ten minutes should say it does, and Copy link and Open link, since a terminal's text can be selected and clicked and this text cannot; Discord's own line says to open the link.
- **A refusal stays in the dialog, in the server's words**: from standard error when it cannot start (`Remote access is off: turn it on in Settings › Remote or with…`, `Tailscale is not installed on this computer`, `Paste the bot's token first`), from `{"paired": false, "error"}` when it stops (`Tailscale is not connected (Stopped): open it and log in`, ten minutes gone), `No phone was paired` when neither says. A toast would go away while the person reads what to do. `Try again` asks again in the same dialog, since cornercase's words for ten minutes gone are `run this again`, and a code that ended or a Tailscale now started needs only that.
- **Once paired it does what the TUI does**: the dialog closes and a toast says so (`A browser connected`, `Telegram connected with Ana`). The row follows by itself: pairing moves the settings' revision, and the sheet reads them again.
- **The QR code** (`ui/qr.rs`) comes from `qrcode` 0.14.1 without its image features, the crate and level (`L`) upstream uses: pure Rust with no dependency of its own, MIT or Apache-2.0. gpui has no such thing, and a matrix has no reason to go through an image. An address too long for a code shows without one, as in the TUI.
- **Drawn to be read by a phone**: black modules on a white ground in both looks of the window, with the standard quiet zone of four modules, inside the ground's rounded corners. Each module is a whole number of device pixels and the origin is snapped to one, whatever the scale and wherever the dialog's centring puts it, so no edge is blurred; a row's dark modules are one quad. The code takes the most modules of whole pixels that fit in 280 px rather than exactly 280 px. `body_height` scrolls the body in a low window, where the TUI says to make the window larger.

## Sharing

- **Export** (`config export --json`) copies the setup as TOML or writes it to a file (`--to=PATH`; on another machine the text is written here instead, since `--to` would write it there, `design/remote.md`); it never holds tokens, accounts or personal keys, and the text is dropped once copied.
- **Import** shows what would change before anything does (#464 upstream, checked with cornercase 0.12.225): the file chosen is read here and sent on standard input to `config import - --preview --json`, which answers each section's changes (`label`, `from`, `to`, as cornercase words them) and notes, and exits without importing. Reading the file here keeps one path for a server on another machine too, whose `config import FILE` would read its own disk. The card lists the sections that change or have notes, risk and secret notes in red with an alert; with nothing to change there is no Import button, and with a risk note the button is the danger one. Importing sends the same text to `config import - --yes --json`, which previews again on the server and applies; the sheet then reads the settings again rather than trusting the answer's shape.
- **Import chosen sections**: each section that changes something has a tick (the TUI's untick, 0.12.177), on by default. An unticked section dims rather than goes, so nothing moves under the pointer and its changes can still be read; it no longer counts in the summary or the button's words, and its risk notes no longer make the button the danger one, while the notes about the whole file do. The import names the rest in `--only=agents,issues` when anything is unticked and nothing otherwise. With nothing ticked the button rests. There is no second preview: the first already holds each section's changes, and `--yes` previews again on the server.
- **A long file name wraps, whole, in the import's header** (#150, 2026-11-12). The card is about one file and Import changes the settings at once, so the name is read whole before answering, as a question's is (`design/widgets.md`, #141); two exports that differ only at their end read alike when cut. As a bare text that could not shrink (`flex_none`), a long name ran past the card and left the summary no width, its words stacked letter by letter. The header (`file_heading`) is now a wrapping row: the icon and the name in one box that shrinks (`min_w_0`), whose text wraps, then the summary at its own width, beside the name when both fit and under it otherwise. A short name sits as before (pixel for pixel at 1280×800); at a narrow width, and whenever a long name fills the line, the summary now goes under the name instead of into a narrow column beside it. Rejected: cutting it with the whole name in a tooltip, as Projects' card header does: that header names one card of a list, beside its folder, and here nothing else names the file.
- **A change's values go under its name when the row is narrow** (#159, 2026-11-13). The row wraps: the label keeps its 190 px and the values start at 240 px (`flex_basis`) and grow into the rest of the line, so below a 440 px row (a window under about 820 px) the values take a line of their own, as wide as the card; from 440 px the row is laid out as before. The values are cornercase's words, not a name: in the 60 px left beside the label at 640 px wide, `[x] shown → [ ] hidden` broke as `sho`/`wn`. A word now breaks mid-word only when it is wider than its line: 240 px beside the label (about 37 characters at 12 px, more than `--dangerously-skip-permissions`), the card's width under it. Rejected: the label giving way (the squeezed part was the values, and labels wrapped into a narrow column read worse than one above its values); a narrower label (labels line up down the card at 190 px); cutting the values (they are what Import changes, read before answering). Checked live on 2026-11-13 (debug builds of 0.1.68 before and 0.1.70 after, gpui 0.2.2, cornercase 0.13.39 on macOS 26.6.2, Apple silicon, a private server), with a temporary build that read the file's path from an environment variable (never committed) and called the real `config import --preview --json` on the private server, seven settings changed: at 640 wide the label is above its values with no word broken and `[x] shown → [ ] hidden` whole (before, one word per line); at about 900 the values sit beside the label and wrap at words. At the window's usual width short values sit where they did, but a long value that fit one line before (`{"claude":…}`) now wraps on two in the narrower column beside the label, which this note does not explain (the layout should give the values the same width as before from a 440 px row); a 1280 px window could not be made here, so the claim that a wide window looks as before is confirmed only for short values. Not checked: Linux, Windows. Seen, also before: at about 900 the last line of a wrapped value touches the next row.
- **Exporting and importing say they run**: Copy becomes `Exporting…` and Import `Importing…`.

## The Discord forum

- **What the feed says, under the Discord rows** (`settings/forum.rs`, `status --app`'s `forum` in Discord's row, cornercase 0.12.192): `Forum ready` with a link to its channel, `Forum not ready:` and cornercase's own words for what is left (`pending`, the first step of the TUI's setup dialog), or `Forum mode off` while a forum is kept. It sits under the `discord_forum` switch when `config --json` lists it, which it does only once a phone is paired, and otherwise under the Discord bot's row, since the mode can be on in `config.json` without one. No `forum` in the feed means nothing is shown.
- **Picking one of several servers** (#668 upstream, cornercase 0.13.12): `forum.servers` comes in the feed exactly while `pending` names the pick, so the list is drawn whenever it is there and no sentence is matched. Under `Forum not ready: pick the server for the forum`, the TUI's words, each server is a row; a click runs `config forum-server ID --json` as a job, the row spins, the other rows take no click, and the answer's name becomes `The forum goes in …`, the command line's words. A refusal (an id no longer listed, no pick waiting, an older server through `--remote`) is the toast. Nothing is kept here: the feed then moves `pending` on and drops `servers`. The rest of the TUI's dialog (the QR code and the link that adds the app to a server) needs the bot's token, which no command gives back, so it stays in cornercase's own window: here each step is its sentence.
