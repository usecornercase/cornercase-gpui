# Architecture

Code: `src/`

## The window and the connection

- `main.rs` starts the app, binds ⌘Q and the text fields' keys, and opens the window on `Root`.
- `app.rs` holds `Root`, the window: the connection (the `view` and `surface` streams), switching servers, the layout, and the hosts for the docked panel, sheets, dialogs and toasts; `app/status.rs` the status bar and the beat that redraws working agents' orbits on the display's frames. `terminal.rs` (keys, ⌘ shortcuts, the surface; `terminal/` holds `mouse`, `selection`, `pane_menu`, `pane_drag` (moving a pane by its handle or the right button: pressing, dragging, dropping with `move-pane`, the handle and the layer the ghost flies in), `input`, `pasting` (⌘V of an image and files dropped on a pane, through `paste-file`), `links` (the path under the pointer: `link --json` asked beside the queue, the underline, the hand, a click that opens it in the Files panel; `Pointer` holds it with the wheel's remainder) and `popup` (a plugin's popup: follows `surface --popup` while the app feed names one, draws it over the terminal, types into it with `input --popup`)), `sidebar.rs` (the sidebar's frame and `card`, the floating card the list and the rail each sit in; `sidebar/` holds `rail` (the card of panel icons left of the sidebar), `look` (the `sidebar`, `tabs` and `agents_section` settings it follows), `arrange` (the cards of each arrangement), `rows`, `workspace` (workspace and agent rows), `tab_rows`, `agents` (the agents section), `menus`, `actions`, `forms` with `forms/style` and `forms/checked`, `order` for drag ordering, and `folds` for folds shown before the session confirms them), `tabs.rs` (the tab bar) and `context_menu.rs` hold the rest of `Root`'s methods. `keymap.rs` is the table of ⌘ shortcuts (commands, keys, labels for tooltips and help), plain data with no window; `dialogs/shortcuts.rs` draws it as the ⌘/ dialog.
- `cli.rs` is everything that talks to cornercase: one-shot commands (`run`), long-running streams (`stream`, a `Stream` stops its process when dropped) and an ordered `Queue` for input. The window never speaks the socket protocol itself. `Target` says where commands run (`connect` changes it); `cli/remote.rs` holds the `--remote`/`--remote-command` flags a `Target::Remote` adds and the reconnect back-off (`design/remote.md`); `cli/typist.rs` the long-running `input --follow --json -` the queue types through; `cli/requests.rs` the long-running `requests --follow -` its commands go through, and how a line and its answer read; `cli/partial.rs` `run_partial`, which takes exit 3 (a whole answer some of whose sources failed) as success. `reason` reads a failed command's error as a sentence, without `cornercase config: error:` around it, for errors a sheet shows in place.
- `model.rs` holds the serde types of `view` (the session: projects, workspaces, tabs, agents) and the readings of it that the sidebar and Search share (`Session::sections`, the sidebar's order; `PaneInfo::agent_title`); `model/pull_request.rs` a workspace's pull request (its words on GitHub or GitLab, its summary and what to tell an agent, its stack's layers and what blocks them, the workspace that holds a layer); `model/surface.rs` those of `surface` (frames, patches, the split tree), `model/grid.rs` the grid they build up, and `model/link.rs` what `link --json` answers (`Link`), the link under the pointer (`Hovered`) and the text of a row's cells.
- `render.rs` draws each pane on its card, its cells with `Metrics` measured from the font; `render/popup.rs` sizes and paints the popup's card; `render/layout.rs` places the cards from the surface's split tree (`Layout`), tells how many cells fit once padding and gaps are taken out, and maps the pointer back to a pane's cells or to the gap of a split; `render/moving.rs` is a pane move as plain data over time (`Moves`: the press, where it would land, the ghost's flight, the cards sliding to their new places, the landing glow), tested without a window, and `render/ghost.rs` paints what it decides (the ghost, the drop zone, its caption and the glow). `keys.rs` encodes gpui keystrokes for `cornercase input`. `theme.rs` holds the terminal palette (also given to cornercase with `start-server --theme`), the chrome colours (`theme::colours`, usecornercase.dev's), the corner radii (`theme::radius`), the terminal's opacity, `over_ground` (a colour that looks over the ground as it would over the desktop) and `FONT_FAMILY`.
- `tour.rs` holds `Guide`, one per `Store` (a global, drawn by `Root` over everything): it follows the app feed's `tour`, opens what a step shows and reports the steps the window completes; `tour/model.rs` the tours' serde types and how steps map to panels, `tour/card.rs` the card, `tour/catalogue.rs` the dialog listing the tours.
- `notify.rs` follows `cornercase events` (`notify/feed.rs` parses them): agent notices as toasts with a "Go" button, macOS notifications, sounds, OSC 52 copies, the bell and the window title. `notify/update.rs` offers updates and restarts.

## Store

`store.rs` holds `Store`, a global gpui entity (`Store::get(cx)`) every part of the window shares: `session` (the view's snapshot), `view_id`, `app` (what `status --app --follow` prints), the open `dock`, `sheet` and `dialog`, the `toasts`, and the `jobs` that run (`store/jobs.rs`: `Busy`, a job's key and what it says, and `Jobs`, the running ones, their summary for the status bar and the workspaces whose worktree is being removed).

- `Store::act(args)` runs a command **with `--view ID`** (focus, new-tab, split, start, send, keys, read, wait, mark, rename, open, new-workspace, go…) through the ordered queue; `Store::cmd(args)` does the same without the view. Errors become toasts.
- `store::run(cx, args, |this, Result<String>, cx| …)` and `store::run_json(…)` run a command off the main thread and give its output back to the entity.
- `store::run_busy(cx, Busy, args, …)` does what `store::run` does while a job says it runs; `Store::act_busy(Busy, args)` is `act` with a job, through the queue; `Store::run_aside(Busy, args)` runs a command beside the queue as a job and toasts its error; `Store::remove_worktree` removes a workspace's worktree that way, and `Store::busy(key)` and `Store::removing(workspace)` tell what runs.
- `store::queued(cx, args, |Result<String>, cx| …)` runs a command through the ordered queue and gives its answer back on the main thread; `dialogs::Question::on_reply` does the same for a confirmation.
- `store::follow(cx, args, |this, line, cx| …) -> Option<Stream>` follows a streaming command, one line per change. The entity keeps the `Stream`; dropping it stops the process.
- `toast(text, ToastKind, cx)`, `ask_agent(workspace, text, label)` (pastes the text into the workspace's agent and focuses it, or copies it when there is none), `copy_elsewhere(path)` (a path on another machine: copied, with a toast that says where it is), `open(kind)`, `toggle_dock(kind)`, `open_in_browser(url, workspace)` (the Browser panel shows it, through `browse`), `run_started(workspace)` (the Flows panel selects the run started there, now or once it is built), `close_sheet()`, `show_dialog(AnyView)`, `close_dialog()`, and `connect(Target)`, which leaves the target in `switch_to` for the window to switch to.

## Panels

`src/panels/` has one module per panel, and `PanelKind` in `mod.rs`. A panel is a gpui entity (`impl Render`) built lazily by `build(window, cx) -> AnyView` and kept alive by `Root`, so it keeps its state while closed. Docked panels (Changes, Files, TODO, Browser, and Flow while the shown workspace runs a flow: `PanelKind::shows`, `Store::shown_dock`) sit right of the terminal (428 px until dragged; the Browser and Flow half the window, up to 720 px, each with a width of its own); the others are sheets over the window: the host draws the title bar and the close button, the panel draws its content and scrolls it with `.id(…).overflow_y_scroll()`, or with gpui's `list` when it is long, since a scrolled div lays out all it holds on every frame (the Changes diff, the Issues list and text, a Flows run; `design/issues.md`, `design/flows.md`). A panel's file keeps its struct, its state changes and `render`; its folder holds the model and parsing (`data.rs`, `model.rs`, `wire.rs`), the commands it runs (`actions.rs`, `load.rs`, `follow.rs`) and the named parts of its drawing (`*_view.rs`, `list.rs`, `detail.rs`, `widgets.rs`, `rows.rs`, and `empty.rs` for the page a panel shows while it holds nothing, as the Queue's). `files/finder.rs` is the ⇧⌘P finder, a dialog over the window that ranks the shown workspace's file names with the Files panel's index and `Source` and opens the chosen one in that panel, which `Root::panel` hands it; `finder/view.rs` draws its field, rows and footer. `files/peek.rs` is `Peek`, the card that shows the file under the pointer (a global entity `Root` draws over the terminal): `peek/body.rs` reads what it shows, `peek/card.rs` draws it, `peek/place.rs` places it beside, below or above the path, `peek/player.rs` plays videos and sounds through AVFoundation and `peek/quicklook.rs` asks Quick Look for a thumbnail of anything else; `files/viewer.rs`'s `open_link` opens a path in the panel at its lines. `flows/start.rs` is the dialog that starts a flow, which the sidebar's project menus and the Issues sheet open too, with `flows::start_flow(FlowStart)` (a project, or an issue and the projects it allows); `start/plan.rs` holds that preset and the `flow run` it builds. `flows/dock.rs` makes the Flow panel, a `FlowsPanel` docked to show the shown workspace's run. `poll.rs` is the one helper shared by panels: `Watch` samples a panel's command every few seconds while it shows (Processes, Ports, the Browser's ports) and counts its openings, so a panel can drop what belonged to an earlier one, `confirm_stop`, `Stop` and `Stopped` ask to stop a process or a port, run it as a job and keep its row stopping until a fresh sample, and `parse` reads a command's JSON into a panel's type.

The Browser panel (`panels/browser.rs`, `design/browser.md`) runs a headless Chrome and draws its screencast. The engine: `browser/chrome.rs` finds a Chromium, its profile and port, launches or attaches to it and ends the one it owns (`browser::quit`, from `main.rs`); `socket.rs` is a minimal WebSocket client; `cdp.rs` the DevTools connection (`Cdp::send`, `Cdp::call`, events); `engine.rs` starts both off the main thread and decodes screencast frames (`frame.rs`, to BGRA) before they reach the panel as `Incoming`. `page.rs` holds the panel's CDP plumbing (`call`, tab adoption, setting up a tab, navigation, the screencast), `tabs.rs` the tabs per workspace and how events move them, `address.rs` what a typed address means, `viewport.rs` the drawn frame, its size in CSS pixels and the pointer's mapping onto the page, and `assets/browser/page.js` the script injected in every page, which talks back through the `cornercaseBridge` binding. Input: `pointer.rs` the viewport's mouse and wheel, one of each in flight; `keyboard.rs` gpui keystrokes to Chrome key events with the Mac editing commands; `typing.rs` keys on the viewport and the input method (`EntityInputHandler for Browser`); `select.rs` `<select>` menus drawn by the window; `input.rs` the input state, the page's caret and select messages, the clipboard and the commands `Root` routes to the browser (`terminal/browser.rs`). Chrome: `toolbar.rs` (back, forward, reload or stop, the address, the tools, new tab, the loading bar), `tab_bar.rs` (the strip from two tabs, labels, titles), `devices.rs` (presets, their metrics, screencast size, touch and user agent), `device_menu.rs` (the popover and the device bar) and `states.rs` (starting and failed, the missing-Chromium help). Agents: `agent.rs` decides which agent gets what (the workspace's agents, the remembered choice, readiness) and hands over through `paste-file`, `input` and `send --enter`; `agent_tools.rs` the toolbar's pick, camera and agents buttons and their menus; `picker.rs` inspect mode → `describe` → crop; `element.rs` the picked element and its description for an agent; `card.rs` the picked card and the picking banner; `shots.rs` screenshots and the PNGs they leave; `watch.rs` reloads a workspace's pages when its agent finishes; `follow.rs` marks and follows the tabs agents open. The page's own voice: `console.rs` the per-tab log of console calls, uncaught errors, the browser's messages and failed requests, and the text handed to an agent (`console/objects.rs` reads a console call's arguments); `console_view.rs` the drawer under the viewport (filters, rows, Send to agent, Clear); `prompts.rs` the card for a page's alert, confirm, prompt and beforeunload; `chooser.rs` the native picker behind a file input; `downloads.rs` downloads moved into `~/Downloads` under a free name; `notices.rs` routes these events, covers a crashed page, remembers popups' openers and holds the panel's own focus handle. Dev servers: `ports.rs` samples the workspace's ports while the panel shows (`impl Polled for Browser`), opens a new one once and routes every open (start page, the Ports sheet's `Store::browse`, auto-open) through `browse`; `start_page.rs` the page shown while a workspace has no tab; `tunnels.rs` an ssh tunnel per port of another machine's workspace (URL rewriting, restarts) and `tunnels/ssh.rs` the ssh processes, forward and reverse, that `browser::quit` stops.

## Widgets

- `ui/mod.rs`: `button`, `primary_button`, `danger_button`, `icon_button`, `status_badge` (a status's icon and colour), `badge` (it drawn in a row, the orbit turning while it works), `section`, `row`, `note`, `dim`, `clamped` (a text at most so many lines tall), `field`, `input_box`, `boxed` (a `TextField` in its box, lit while focused), `search_field` (one with the search icon and an optional clear button), `checkbox`, `segment(s)`, `title`, `spinner` (the loader icon, turning), `busy_button` (a button's place while its work runs, in its tone), `busy` (a small spinner and text), `loading` (a panel's note with a spinner), `tool_button` (a rounded icon button, tinted when active), `icon_text_button` (an icon and a label in a `Tone`: plain, primary or danger), `undo_bar` (a removal's text and its Undo, as TODO and Queue show it), `labelled_button` (one of those stretched to share a row), `bar` (a capsule filled to a share, as Usage's limits and Processes' CPU draw it), `emblem` (a panel's icon in a cobalt disc, heading a page that explains it: Issues without a tracker, the empty Queue), `resting_button` (a button shown before it can be pressed), `home_relative` (a path with the home folder as `~`) and `anonymous_element!` (the `id` and `source_location` gpui's `Element` asks of an element that has neither).
- `ui/signs.rs`: what tells a state, shared by sheets: `accent_chip`, `quiet_chip`, `tinted_chip`, the status `dot`, `error_line`, `warning_line`, `done_line`, `callout`, `row_action` / `resting_action` (an icon with a tooltip in a row's fixed slot, `ROW_ACTION` wide) and `field_action` (a small icon button inside a field).
- `ui/glass.rs`: the look's surfaces and effects: `card(radius)` (a floating card), `popover` (a tooltip), `dragged` (a drag preview), `glow` (a coloured shadow), `ring` (a focus ring), `cobalt` (the brand's gradient fill), `lit` (a selected row's cobalt tint), `wash` (the light at the top of the rail's and the sidebar's cards) and `tint`.
- `ui/brand.rs`: `mark` (the corner and cursor, at any size, its cursor blinking while connecting) and `wordmark`.
- `ui/icons.rs`: `Icon`, the SVGs in `assets/icons/` embedded with `include_bytes!` and served to gpui by `Assets` (given to `Application::with_assets`); draw one with `icon(Icon::Files, 16.)`, coloured by `text_color`.
- `ui/file_icons.rs`: a file's or folder's icon by its type, `file_icon(name, dir, open, size)`, tinted (the TUI's table, the SVGs in `assets/file-icons/`); `Assets` serves them too.
- `ui/split.rs`: `Split`, a size a drag chooses within limits (the default until then), and the two halves of a draggable border: `handle` (the strip on the line, its cursor, its double click) and `follow` (the container's `on_drag_move`, which moves the size with the pointer).
- `ui/motion.rs`: `slide`, `fade`, `veil` (a backdrop whose colour fades), `spin` (turns an `svg`) and `pulse` wrap an element in gpui's `with_animation`; `orbit` turns the working icon to the wall clock's angle (`orbit_turned`) and counts the orbits a frame drew (`orbits_drawn`), and `Beat` decides on each display frame whether the orbits need another one (`next_frame`); with `Way::{In, Pop, Out}` setting duration and easing; `Leaving<T>` keeps a closed sheet, dialog or dock on screen while it animates out.
- `ui/one_line.rs`: `one_line(text)`, a text kept to one line that ends in "…" where its box ends; `.highlights(…)` keeps a search's matches or a file's colours.
- `ui/text_field.rs`: `TextField`, an entity (`cx.new(|cx| TextField::new("placeholder", cx))`) that emits `TextFieldEvent::{Changed, Submit, Cancel}`, with `text()` and `set_text()`, and with `reverts_on_escape()` the value it was saved with (`keep_saved`, `edited`, `revert`); focus it with `window.focus(&field.read(cx).focus_handle(cx))`.
- `dialogs.rs`: `frame(…)` for dialogs, `show` to open one and focus it, `confirm(…)` for anything that cannot be undone (its `Question` queues a command, runs it beside the queue as a job with `busy`, gives its answer back with `on_reply`, or with `on_yes` runs a closure instead), `new_workspace`, `start_agent`. `dialogs/pull_request.rs` is the pull request dialog (`pull_request(workspace)`, `shown_pull_request` for ⌘P), with `pull_request/signs.rs` (the row's tags, and the signs and colours of checks) and `pull_request/stack.rs` (its stack section). `dialogs/remote.rs` holds `connect` (the ⇧⌘K dialog, with `remote/hosts.rs` reading `~/.ssh/config`) and `ask_path`, which asks for a path on another machine.

gpui 0.2.2's sources and examples are in `~/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-*/gpui-0.2.2/`.
