# Architecture

Code: `src/`

## The window and the connection

- `main.rs` starts the app, binds the system keys (⌘Q, Ctrl+Shift+Q on Linux) and the text fields' keys, and opens the window on `Root`.
- `platform.rs` holds `Os`, the platform as a value (`Os::CURRENT`), so what differs between macOS, Linux and Windows is plain logic tested for each on any host, and `command`, the one way to make a child process (on Windows without a console window of its own, `design/windows.md`); `platform/paths.rs` the rules of a path's text on either kind of machine (`Style`: Unix's or Windows', from an `Os` or from the path's own form; its name, folder, separator, joining and showing it) and the home folder as `~` (`home_relative`), so the window reads a server's paths by that server's rules (`design/remote.md`).
- `app.rs` holds `Root`, the window: its connection, switching servers, the layout, and the hosts for the docked panel, sheets, dialogs and toasts; `app/connection.rs` a connection's fields and streams (`Connection`: its queue, `view` and `surface`, grid and size, and whether a line is still its own), one for the window (`Root::own`) and one for a machine's tab; `app/machine.rs` a machine's tab (`MachineTab`, `Root::shown`, what each stream end does, keys, steering the view, colours); `app/toasts.rs` the toasts' stack and what a toast's button does; `app/status.rs` the status bar and the beat that redraws working agents' orbits on the display's frames; `app/surface.rs` the window's own `surface` stream: opening it once the view shows a tab, reopening it, and waiting while the view shows none or a machine's tab shows; `app/frame.rs` the frame the window draws itself on Linux (`Frame`, read from gpui's `Decorations`: the margin, corners, line and shadow, the resize grips, the title row that moves and zooms the window, and the minimise, maximise and close buttons), the window options that ask for it, and fitting the window to a window manager's frame (`fit_to_frame`, `min_size`); `app/frame/win32.rs` asks DWM for Windows 11's acrylic backdrop (`no_win32.rs` elsewhere); `app/dock.rs` the docked panel's card, and placing it over the terminal for a file's full screen. `terminal.rs` (keys, ⌘ shortcuts, the surface; `terminal/` holds `machine_card` (what the terminal's place shows while a machine's workspace is picked, `Root::machine_tab_area`, and the one place told of a change, `machine_place_changed`), `mouse`, `selection`, `pane_menu`, `pane_accounts` (the account chips in a card's bottom padding and the menu of accounts they and `Switch account` open), `pane_notes` (the band of a held message and `↓ n more`), `steps` (the keys that go to the next workspace, project or agent that needs you, and the mark), `pane_drag` (moving a pane by its handle or the right button: pressing, dragging, dropping with `move-pane`, the handle and the layer the ghost flies in), `input`, `pasting` (⌘V of an image and files dropped on a pane, through `paste-file`), `links` (the path or web address under the pointer: `link --json` asked beside the queue for a path, an address found in the row itself, the underline, the hand, a click that opens a path in the Files panel and an address in the Browser; `Pointer` holds it with the wheel's remainder) `popup` (a plugin's popup: follows `surface --popup` while the app feed names one, draws it over the terminal, types into it with `input --popup`) and `no_tab` (the terminal's place while the view shows no tab: "No tab open" with its buttons, or the welcome when there is no project)), `sidebar.rs` (the sidebar's frame and `card`, the floating card the list and the rail each sit in; `sidebar/` holds `rail` (the card of panel icons left of the sidebar), `look` (the `sidebar`, `tabs`, `agents_section`, `account_mark` and `account_switcher` settings it follows), `arrange` (the cards of each arrangement), `rows`, `workspace` (workspace and agent rows), `tab_rows`, `agents` (the agents section), `machine_rows` (a machine's workspaces, tabs and projects, and their folds), `machine_agents` (a machine's rows in the agents section), `machine_acts` (its menus, rename and close), `menus`, `actions`, `opening` (opening a project, importing a VS Code workspace), `forms` with `forms/style` and `forms/checked`, `order` for drag ordering, and `folds` for folds shown before the session confirms them), `tabs.rs` (the tab bar; `tabs/strip.rs` scrolls it when the tabs do not fit and keeps the active one in view) and `context_menu.rs` hold the rest of `Root`'s methods. `keymap.rs` is the table of ⌘ shortcuts (commands, keys, labels for tooltips and help) and how a keystroke reads as one on each platform (`chord`: Ctrl+Shift on Linux), plain data with no window; `dialogs/shortcuts.rs` draws it as the ⌘/ dialog.
- `cli.rs` is everything that talks to cornercase: one-shot commands (`run`, or `run_on` a given `Target`, as a machine's; `run_partial` and `run_partial_on` also take exit 3 as an answer), long-running streams (`stream_on`, a `Stream` stops its process when dropped) and an ordered `Queue` for input (`Queue::new` on the window's target, `Queue::on` on a machine's, `Queue::nowhere` while it is down), which tells the window when a command moves its view (`moves_view`). The window never speaks the socket protocol itself. `Target` says where commands run (`connect` changes it); `cli/remote.rs` holds the `--remote`/`--remote-command` flags a `Target::Remote` adds and the reconnect back-off (`design/remote.md`); `cli/locate.rs` finds the binary (the `PATH`, the login shell's, the installers' folders) and checks its version, with `cli/locate/system.rs` running the processes; `cli/typist.rs` the long-running `input --follow --json -` the queue types through; `cli/requests.rs` the long-running `requests --follow -` its commands go through, and how a line and its answer read; `cli/window_processes.rs` the one `plugin windows --json` the window keeps while it is on this computer, stopped with a signal rather than killed (`design/plugins.md`); `cli/partial.rs` `run_partial`, which takes exit 3 (a whole answer some of whose sources failed) as success, and `run_printed`, which keeps what a command printed before it failed (`plugin run`'s outcome). `reason` reads a failed command's error as a sentence, without `cornercase config: error:` around it, for errors a sheet shows in place.
- `model.rs` holds the serde types of `view` (the session: projects, workspaces, tabs, agents) and the readings of it that the sidebar and Search share (`Session::sections`, the sidebar's order; `PaneInfo::agent_title`; `Session::path_style`, the rules of the server's paths); `model/pull_request.rs` a workspace's pull request (its words on GitHub or GitLab, its summary and what to tell an agent, every check once `pull-request --json` gave them, its stack's layers and what blocks them, the workspace that holds a layer); `model/shared.rs` the files a workspace shares with others (how many, what its row and a file's tooltip say); `model/tree.rs` the walk that makes agents a tree, for any key; `model/machines/seen/agents.rs` the agents section's rows, ours and each machine's, in order, and a fold aimed at a pane there (`PaneAim`, `sighted_pane`); `model/machines/seen.rs` what a `view` line says of each machine (`MachineSeen`, `ThereProject`, `ThereWorkspace`, `ThereId`, whose ids are another server's; `Aim` and the commands on a workspace there; `MachinePlace`, a picked place, and how it follows a snapshot; and the `Session` readings that place rows); `model/machines.rs` what `machines --json` and `status --app` say of the machines (`Machine`, its `LinkState` and `MachineIcon`, its state in words, `in_app`, the one parser of the list), for whatever shows them; `model/details.rs` what a tab's row and the keys read from the session (`Tab::memory`, `memory_size`, `Session::project_order`, `Session::agent_needing_you`); `model/agent_context.rs` what `context --json` answers (`ContextAnswer`, its `Breakdown`, a category's place by its kind, the tools that took the most); `model/accounts.rs` what `account list --json` answers, shared with the Accounts sheet, the defaults of `status --app` (`Selection`) and their readings for a pane (its mark, its switchers, each switch's command and what its answer says); `model/accounts/pick.rs` what a form that starts an agent can offer (`AgentChoices`), the state of one choice (`Picking`) and what it sends (`AccountPick`, `account_args`); `model/surface.rs` those of `surface` (frames, patches, the split tree), `model/grid.rs` the grid they build up, and `model/link.rs` what `link --json` answers (`Link`), the link under the pointer (`Hovered`, its `Span` and its `Target`, a file or a web address), the web addresses of a row (`url_in`, `web_hyperlink`) and the text of a row's cells.
- `render.rs` draws each pane on its card, its cells with `Metrics` measured from the font; `render/popup.rs` sizes and paints the popup's card; `render/layout.rs` places the cards from the surface's split tree (`Layout`), tells how many cells fit once padding and gaps are taken out, and maps the pointer back to a pane's cells or to the gap of a split; `render/moving.rs` is a pane move as plain data over time (`Moves`: the press, where it would land, the ghost's flight, the cards sliding to their new places, the landing glow), tested without a window, and `render/ghost.rs` paints what it decides (the ghost, the drop zone, its caption and the glow). `keys.rs` encodes gpui keystrokes for `cornercase input`. `theme.rs` holds the terminal palette (also given to cornercase with `start-server --theme`), the chrome colours (`theme::colours`, usecornercase.dev's), the corner radii (`theme::radius`), the terminal's opacity, `over_ground` (a colour that looks over the ground as it would over the desktop), the ground per platform (`ground`), and `FONT_FAMILY` and `UI_FAMILY`, which `theme/fonts.rs` resolves at start to fonts installed on Linux.
- `tour.rs` holds `Guide`, one per `Store` (a global, drawn by `Root` over everything): it follows the app feed's `tour`, opens what a step shows and reports the steps the window completes; `tour/model.rs` the tours' serde types and how steps map to panels, `tour/card.rs` the card, `tour/catalogue.rs` the Help dialog: the docs and bug links, the tours, resetting their progress.
- `notify.rs` follows `cornercase events` (`notify/feed.rs` parses them): agent notices as toasts with a "Go" button, desktop notifications, sounds, OSC 52 copies, the bell and the window title. `notify/alert.rs` builds the notification and the sound per platform (`osascript` and `afplay`, `notify-send` and `paplay` with the freedesktop sounds, or a Windows toast and `PlaySound` with Windows' own sounds), and runs them; `notify/alert/win32.rs` holds the Windows calls (`no_win32.rs` their stubs elsewhere). `notify/update.rs` offers cornercase's updates and restarts (`Chip`, and `installed`, which also counts the `cornercase` on disk).
- `self_update.rs` is the window's own update: `Updater`, a global entity made in `main.rs` that asks GitHub for the latest release at start and every hour, keeps what it found, installs it and prepares the relaunch (`self_update/`: `release` reads GitHub's answer, compares versions, picks the notes, the asset and checks a checksum, `fetch` is the only file that uses `ureq`, `install` decides how this copy updates and does it, `bundle` is macOS's part of it (the app around the program, the DMG, the signature check, the swap and the relaunch through `open`), `relaunch` is what starts the new program, `dialog` the dialog). `main.rs` also answers `--version`. `platform/probe.rs` runs a program for a few lines of its output within a time limit (the `cornercase --version` check and the downloaded program's).
- `desktop.rs` is what the desktop the window runs on offers: its name for this machine (`machine`, `here`: "this Mac", "this computer" or "this PC"), its file manager's name and menu labels, opening a path with its default app (`open`) and showing it in the file manager (`reveal`) through `open`, `xdg-open`, the freedesktop `FileManager1` D-Bus call or Windows' shell (the Win32 calls in `desktop/win32.rs`, which also names the app for the taskbar, with stubs in `desktop/no_win32.rs` elsewhere), what counts as a program that opening would run (`runs_when_opened`), running such a tool with a deadline and its last words as the error (`run`), private temporary folders and files (`Scratch`, which goes when dropped), finding tools (`Lookup`, through `PATHEXT` on Windows), the XDG data folders and the Windows folder, and the primary selection on Linux (`Board`, where a finished selection goes on each platform, `read_primary`, `write_primary`).

## Store

`terminal/missing.rs` draws the page for a missing or unusable cornercase and lets the user retry or choose a binary, kept in `settings.json` as `cornercase_bin`.

`store.rs` holds `Store`, a global gpui entity (`Store::get(cx)`) every part of the window shares: `session` (the view's snapshot), `view_id`, `app` (what `status --app --follow` prints), `accounts` (what `account list --json` said last, asked again when `status --app`'s `accounts_revision` or the view's config revision moves, `accounts_changes` counting what changed), `selection` (the defaults in `app`), `account_settings` (`account_mark`, `account_picker` and the default agent of `config --json`, handed over by `sidebar/look.rs`; `account_choices` is what forms offer), `machines` (its `machines`, parsed there once, which every change's `--json` answer replaces through `take_machines`, `design/machines.md`), the open `dock`, `sheet` and `dialog`, the `toasts`, `openers` (the apps of this machine that open a path, found once off the main thread, `files/open.rs`), the window's own settings (`prefs`: `prefs.rs` reads and writes them, `Store::set_pref` changes one, `toggle_long_lines` the one the viewer's and the diff's button share), whether a file has the terminal's area (`file_full_screen`, `shows_file_full_screen`, `design/files-panel.md`), what the plugins offer the window (`plugins`, `panels/plugins/offers.rs`), and the `jobs` that run (`store/jobs.rs`: `Busy`, a job's key and what it says, and `Jobs`, the running ones, their summary for the status bar and the workspaces whose worktree is being removed, here or on a machine), and `machine_place`, a machine's workspace picked in the sidebar; `store/machines_there.rs` holds what acts on a machine: `machine_target` (whether and how it can be reached from this window), `reach_machine`, `act_on_machine` with `MachineAct`, `pick_machine_place` and `leave_machine_place`, a notice's `go_there`, and its home (`design/machines.md`).

- `Store::act(args)` runs a command **with `--view ID`** (focus, new-tab, split, start, send, keys, read, wait, mark, rename, open, new-workspace, go…) through the ordered queue; `Store::cmd(args)` does the same without the view. Errors become toasts.
- `store::run(cx, args, |this, Result<String>, cx| …)` and `store::run_json(…)` run a command off the main thread and give its output back to the entity.
- `store::run_on(cx, Target, args, …)` and `store::follow_on(cx, &Target, args, …)` run or follow a command on a given target, a machine's; `store::follow` and `follow_until` are the latter on the window's own.
- `Store::machine_read()` (`store/machine_read.rs`) says what Changes and Files read while a machine's workspace is picked: its `Aim`, its path and how it is reached (`Reach`).
- `store::run_busy(cx, Busy, args, …)` does what `store::run` does while a job says it runs, and `store::run_partial_busy` what `store::run_partial` does; `Store::act_busy(Busy, args)` is `act` with a job, through the queue; `Store::run_aside(Busy, args)` runs a command beside the queue as a job and toasts its error; `Store::start_here(StartHere)` runs a `start --placed` of the shown dialog that way, keeps that dialog on `dialogs::dialog_starts` until it answers and then closes it and the sheet, as `Store::start_there` does for a machine; `Store::switch_account(Switch)` runs a pane's `account restart` that way and toasts what it answered; `Store::remove_worktree` removes a workspace's worktree that way, and `Store::busy(key)` and `Store::removing(workspace)` tell what runs; `store::run_local(cx, Busy, work)` runs work of this machine rather than a command (opening a file in an app) off the main thread as a job and toasts its error; `store::run_local_busy(cx, Busy, work, then)` is that work as a job whose result comes back to `then` (the window's own update).
- `store::queued(cx, args, |Result<String>, cx| …)` runs a command through the ordered queue and gives its answer back on the main thread; `dialogs::Question::on_reply` does the same for a confirmation.
- `store::follow(cx, args, |this, line, cx| …) -> Option<Stream>` follows a streaming command, one line per change. The entity keeps the `Stream`; dropping it stops the process.
- `toast(text, ToastKind, cx)`, `ask_agent(workspace, text, label)` (pastes the text into the workspace's agent, focuses it and gives it the keys, or copies it when there is none), `ask_about(workspace, reference)` (the same with a `path:a-b ` reference), `open_in_editor(workspace, path, line)` (a new tab running the editor there), `show_in_terminal(kind, id)` (focuses a pane, workspace or project and gives the keys to the terminal, which `Root` does on seeing `keys_to_terminal`), `copy_elsewhere(path)` (a path on another machine: copied, with a toast that says where it is), `give_keys_back()` (the same without moving the focus elsewhere), `open(kind)`, `toggle_dock(kind)`, `open_on_project(kind, project)` (a sheet that shows that project, which takes it with `take_project_sheet`), `read_run_file(path)` (the Files panel shows a run's file and goes back to the Flow panel), `open_in_browser(url, workspace)` (the Browser panel shows it, through `browse`), `run_started(workspace)` (the Flows panel selects the run started there, now or once it is built), `close_sheet()`, `show_dialog(AnyView)`, `close_dialog()`, and `connect(Target)`, which leaves the target in `switch_to` for the window to switch to.

## Panels

`src/panels/` has one module per panel, and `PanelKind` in `mod.rs`; `on_machine.rs` holds what Changes and Files share on a machine's workspace (the strip that names it, `Retry`), `files/there.rs` what Files asks there. A panel is a gpui entity (`impl Render`) built lazily by `build(window, cx) -> AnyView` and kept alive by `Root`, so it keeps its state while closed. Docked panels (Changes, Files, TODO, Recap, Browser, and Flow while the shown workspace runs a flow: `PanelKind::shows`, `Store::shown_dock`) sit right of the terminal (428 px until dragged; the Browser and Flow half the window, up to 720 px, each with a width of its own); the others are sheets over the window: the host draws the title bar and the close button, the panel draws its content and scrolls it with `.id(…).overflow_y_scroll()`, or with gpui's `list` when it is long, since a scrolled div lays out all it holds on every frame (the Changes diff, the Issues list and text, a Flows run; `design/issues.md`, `design/flows.md`). A panel's file keeps its struct, its state changes and `render`; its folder holds the model and parsing (`data.rs`, `model.rs`, `wire.rs`), the commands it runs (`actions.rs`, `load.rs`, `follow.rs`) and the named parts of its drawing (`*_view.rs`, `list.rs`, `detail.rs`, `widgets.rs`, `rows.rs`, and `empty.rs` for the page a panel shows while it holds nothing, as the Queue's). `recap/` holds `data` (the answer of `recap --json`, a range's `Slot`), `names` (naming the ids in the Markdown, read with Issues' `markdown.rs` and drawn with its `text.rs`), `links` (where a link goes) and `view`. `search/settings.rs` asks for the settings that match and draws their rows; choosing one hands it to `settings/landing.rs` through `Store::land_on`, which opens the sheet on that row. `changes/filter.rs` is the file list's path filter, as plain functions. `files/open.rs` finds the apps that open a path and runs them (`Opening`), for the panel and for the sidebar's and the pane's menus. `files/gutter.rs` holds the git marks of the viewer's margin as plain logic (`Gutter`, `Margin`) and `files/gutter_view.rs` reads and draws them, with the tree's marks. `files/finder.rs` is the ⇧⌘P finder, a dialog over the window that ranks the shown workspace's file names with the Files panel's index and `Source` and opens the chosen one in that panel, which `Root::panel` hands it; `finder/view.rs` draws its field, rows and footer. `files/peek.rs` is `Peek`, the card that shows the file or web page under the pointer (a global entity `Root` draws over the terminal): `peek/body.rs` reads what it shows, `peek/card.rs` draws it, `peek/web.rs` asks the Browser panel for a page's picture and draws its header and its Show the page, `peek/place.rs` places it beside, below or above the path, `peek/player.rs` plays videos and sounds through AVFoundation (on Linux `peek/no_player.rs` stands in for it and plays nothing), `peek/quicklook.rs` asks Quick Look for a thumbnail of anything else, `peek/thumbnailers.rs` asks the freedesktop thumbnailers on Linux, and `peek/shell.rs` asks the Windows shell for the thumbnail File Explorer shows; `files/viewer.rs`'s `open_link` opens a path in the panel at its lines. `flows/start.rs` is the dialog that starts a flow, which the sidebar's project menus and the Issues sheet open too, with `flows::start_flow(FlowStart)` (a project, or an issue and the projects it allows); `start/plan.rs` holds that preset and the `flow run` it builds. `flows/dock.rs` makes the Flow panel, a `FlowsPanel` docked to show the shown workspace's run. `poll.rs` is the one helper shared by panels: `Watch` samples a panel's command every few seconds while it shows (Processes, Ports, the Browser's ports) and counts its openings, so a panel can drop what belonged to an earlier one, `confirm_stop`, `Stop` and `Stopped` ask to stop a process or a port, run it as a job and keep its row stopping until a fresh sample, and `parse` reads a command's JSON into a panel's type.

The Browser panel (`panels/browser.rs`, `design/browser.md`) runs a headless Chrome and draws its screencast. The engine: `browser/chrome.rs` finds a Chromium, its profile and port, launches or attaches to it and ends the one it owns (`browser::quit`, from `main.rs`); `win32.rs`, on Windows only, its Win32 calls (whether a process lives, the process behind a profile's singleton window, the Downloads folder); `socket.rs` is a minimal WebSocket client; `cdp.rs` the DevTools connection (`Cdp::send`, `Cdp::call`, events); `engine.rs` starts both off the main thread and decodes screencast frames (`frame.rs`, to BGRA) before they reach the panel as `Incoming`. `page.rs` holds the panel's CDP plumbing (`call`, tab adoption, setting up a tab, navigation, the screencast), `tabs.rs` the tabs per workspace and how events move them, `address.rs` what a typed address means, `viewport.rs` the drawn frame, its size in CSS pixels and the pointer's mapping onto the page, and `assets/browser/page.js` the script injected in every page, which talks back through the `cornercaseBridge` binding. Input: `pointer.rs` the viewport's mouse and wheel, one of each in flight; `keyboard.rs` gpui keystrokes to Chrome key events, with the Mac editing commands on macOS; `typing.rs` keys on the viewport and the input method (`EntityInputHandler for Browser`); `select.rs` `<select>` menus drawn by the window; `input.rs` the input state, the page's caret and select messages, the clipboard and the commands `Root` routes to the browser (`terminal/browser.rs`). Chrome: `toolbar.rs` (back, forward, reload or stop, the address, the tools, new tab, the loading bar), `tab_bar.rs` (the strip from two tabs, labels, titles), `devices.rs` (presets, their metrics, screencast size, touch and user agent), `device_menu.rs` (the popover and the device bar) and `states.rs` (starting and failed, the missing-Chromium help). Agents: `agent.rs` decides which agent gets what (the workspace's agents, the remembered choice, readiness) and hands over through `paste-file`, `input` and `send --enter`; `agent_tools.rs` the toolbar's pick, camera and agents buttons and their menus; `picker.rs` inspect mode → `describe` → crop; `element.rs` the picked element and its description for an agent; `card.rs` the picked card and the picking banner; `shots.rs` screenshots and the PNGs they leave; `watch.rs` reloads a workspace's pages when its agent finishes; `follow.rs` marks and follows the tabs agents open. The page's own voice: `console.rs` the per-tab log of console calls, uncaught errors, the browser's messages and failed requests, and the text handed to an agent (`console/objects.rs` reads a console call's arguments); `console_view.rs` the drawer under the viewport (filters, rows, Send to agent, Clear); `prompts.rs` the card for a page's alert, confirm, prompt and beforeunload; `chooser.rs` the native picker behind a file input; `downloads.rs` downloads moved into the Downloads folder under a free name; `notices.rs` routes these events, covers a crashed page, remembers popups' openers and holds the panel's own focus handle. Dev servers: `ports.rs` samples the workspace's ports while the panel shows (`impl Polled for Browser`), opens a new one once and routes every open (start page, the Ports sheet's `Store::browse`, auto-open) through `browse`; `start_page.rs` the page shown while a workspace has no tab; `tunnels.rs` an ssh tunnel per port of another machine's workspace (URL rewriting, restarts) and `tunnels/ssh.rs` the ssh processes, forward and reverse, that `browser::quit` stops. `preview.rs` takes the picture of a page for the terminal's card (`Browser::preview`), in a browser context of its own. The panel is one entity per window, `browser::shared`, so the card reaches the same one `Root` docks (`browser::running` when it must not start Chrome).

## Widgets

- `ui/mod.rs`: `button`, `primary_button`, `danger_button`, `icon_button`, `status_badge` (a status's icon and colour), `badge` (it drawn in a row, the orbit turning while it works), `still_badge` (the same, never turning, for a machine that is down), `section`, `row`, `note`, `dim`, `clamped` (a text at most so many lines tall), `field`, `input_box`, `boxed` (a `TextField` in its box, lit while focused), `search_field` (one with the search icon and an optional clear button), `checkbox` (`tick_box` is its box alone), `segment(s)`, `title`, `spinner` (the loader icon, turning), `busy_button` (a button's place while its work runs, in its tone), `busy` (a small spinner and text), `loading` (a panel's note with a spinner), `tool_button` (a rounded icon button, tinted when active), `letter_button` (one with a character instead of an icon), `busy_tool` (their place while their work runs), `icon_text_button` (an icon and a label in a `Tone`: plain, primary or danger), `undo_bar` (a removal's text and its Undo, as TODO and Queue show it), `labelled_button` (one of those stretched to share a row), `bar` (a capsule filled to a share, as Usage's limits and Processes' CPU draw it), `emblem` (a panel's icon in a cobalt disc, heading a page that explains it: Issues without a tracker, the empty Queue), `resting_button` (a button shown before it can be pressed), `home_relative` (a path with this machine's home folder as `~`, by `platform::paths`) and `anonymous_element!` (the `id` and `source_location` gpui's `Element` asks of an element that has neither).
- `ui/signs.rs`: what tells a state, shared by sheets: `accent_chip`, `quiet_chip`, `tinted_chip`, the status `dot`, `error_line`, `warning_line`, `done_line`, `callout`, `row_action` / `resting_action` (an icon with a tooltip in a row's fixed slot, `ROW_ACTION` wide) and `field_action` (a small icon button inside a field).
- `ui/glass.rs`: the look's surfaces and effects: `card(radius)` (a floating card), `popover` (a tooltip), `dragged` (a drag preview), `glow` (a coloured shadow), `ring` (a focus ring), `cobalt` (the brand's gradient fill), `lit` (a selected row's cobalt tint), `wash` (the light at the top of the rail's and the sidebar's cards) and `tint`.
- `ui/account_picker.rs`: `AccountPicker`, a dropdown of an agent's accounts (an entity), and `AccountPickers`, a form's set of them, one per agent, given the list (`follow`) and asked for the rows of the agents it starts (`fields`) and what they send (`picks`). The Issues start, `Start an agent`, the Queue's form and `Start a flow` use it (`design/accounts.md`).
- `ui/menu.rs`: a pop-up's pieces, shared by Settings' choices, a flow start's project and the account picker: `dropdown` (the button, its `edge` given) and `chevron`, `backdrop` (the layer that takes the press that closes it), `card` (the menu, focused and scrolled), `entry` (a row with its check's slot, a cobalt pill when highlighted) and `inks`, and the highlight's moves, `step` (↑ and ↓) and `hovered` (the pointer).
- `ui/brand.rs`: `mark` (the corner and cursor, at any size, its cursor blinking while connecting) and `wordmark`.
- `ui/icons.rs`: `Icon`, the SVGs in `assets/icons/` embedded with `include_bytes!` and served to gpui by `Assets` (given to `Application::with_assets`); draw one with `icon(Icon::Files, 16.)`, coloured by `text_color`.
- `ui/file_icons.rs`: a file's or folder's icon by its type, `file_icon(name, dir, open, size)`, tinted (the TUI's table, the SVGs in `assets/file-icons/`); `Assets` serves them too.
- `ui/split.rs`: `Split`, a size a drag chooses within limits (the default until then), and the two halves of a draggable border: `handle` (the strip on the line, its cursor, its double click) and `follow` (the container's `on_drag_move`, which moves the size with the pointer).
- `ui/motion.rs`: `slide`, `fade`, `veil` (a backdrop whose colour fades), `spin` (turns an `svg`) and `pulse` wrap an element in gpui's `with_animation`; `orbit` turns the working icon to the wall clock's angle (`orbit_turned`) and counts the orbits a frame drew (`orbits_drawn`), and `Beat` decides on each display frame whether the orbits need another one (`next_frame`); with `Way::{In, Pop, Out}` setting duration and easing; `Leaving<T>` keeps a closed sheet, dialog or dock on screen while it animates out.
- `ui/qr.rs`: `Matrix::of(text)`, a QR code's modules, and `code(matrix, room, window)`, which draws them on a light ground with a quiet zone, each module a whole number of device pixels.
- `ui/one_line.rs`: `one_line(text)`, a text kept to one line that ends in "…" where its box ends; `.highlights(…)` keeps a search's matches or a file's colours; `.on_laid(…)` says where it was laid out and cut, for a hit test.
- `ui/selection.rs`: selecting lines and characters in a text that is no field, shared by the Files viewer and the Changes diff: `Selection<S>`, `Press<S>`, `overlay` (paints ranges over a line's styles), where the floating bar goes, `own_part` (what a line holding tabs has under the range drawn), and the `reference` and label of a range of lines, all plain logic; `ui/selection/pointer.rs` the rest: `Selecting<S>`, `Laid<S>`, `Selects` (what a panel implements), `body` (the box that takes the press), `layer`, `row_probe`, `action`, `action_bar`; `ui/selection/code.rs` the line both draw, wrapped or cut: `Fit`, `code_cell`, `plain_text`, `wrap_button`.
- `ui/text_field.rs`: `TextField`, an entity (`cx.new(|cx| TextField::new("placeholder", cx))`) that emits `TextFieldEvent::{Changed, Submit, Cancel}`, with `text()` and `set_text()`, `wraps()` for one line that breaks into rows and `multiline(rows)` for several lines that grow, then scroll (`inside(scroller)` keeps the caret in view in the form around it); `text_field/element.rs` lays it out and draws it, `text_field/rows.rs` holds its rows and scrolling as plain numbers; and with `reverts_on_escape()` the value it was saved with (`keep_saved`, `edited`, `revert`); focus it with `window.focus(&field.read(cx).focus_handle(cx))`.
- `dialogs/pairing.rs`: the dialog that pairs a phone (`pairing(key)`), which follows `config pair KEY --json` while it is open, with `pairing/answer.rs` for what its lines say, tested without a window.
- `dialogs.rs`: `frame(…)` for dialogs, `show` to open one and focus it, `confirm(…)` for anything that cannot be undone (its `Question` queues a command, runs it beside the queue as a job with `busy`, gives its answer back with `on_reply`, or with `on_yes` runs a closure instead; `body` draws its words in place of the message, and `tick` adds a box that changes them, the button and what yes does), `new_workspace`, `start_agent`. `dialogs/pull_request.rs` is the pull request dialog (`pull_request(workspace)`, `shown_pull_request` for ⌘P), with `pull_request/read.rs` (asking `pull-request --json` for every check, which answer counts and when to ask again), `pull_request/signs.rs` (the row's tags, and the signs, colours and lines of checks) and `pull_request/stack.rs` (its stack section). `dialogs/merge_check.rs` is the merge check (`merge_check(workspace)`), with `merge_check/answer.rs` (reading `changes merge-check --json`, its lines and what to tell an agent, as plain data). `dialogs/context.rs` is the Context dialog (`view_context(pane)`, `context_choices(tab)` for a tab's menu), with `context/read.rs` (`context --cached` then a read, status 3, what it says of them) and `context/lists.rs` (the slices and rows made from an answer). `dialogs/remote.rs` holds `connect` (the ⇧⌘K dialog, with `remote/hosts.rs` reading `~/.ssh/config`) and `ask_path`, which asks for a path on another machine. `dialogs/run_on.rs` is `RunOn`, the Run on row of the dialogs that start work (an entity of its own: the chips, asking the machine, `gate`, `where_line`, `agent_line`), with `run_on/clone.rs` the Clone form; their logic is plain data in `model/machines/run_on.rs`, `run_on/checks.rs` and `run_on/clone.rs`, and the jobs and the selection after a start in `store/run_on.rs` (`design/machines.md`).

gpui 0.2.2's sources and examples are in `~/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-*/gpui-0.2.2/`.
