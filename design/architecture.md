# Architecture

Code: `src/`

## The window and the connection

- `main.rs` starts the app, binds the system keys (⌘Q, Ctrl+Shift+Q on Linux) and the text fields' keys, and opens the window on `Root` where the last one was (`app/placement.rs`).
- `platform.rs` holds `Os`, the platform as a value (`Os::CURRENT`), so what differs between macOS, Linux and Windows is plain logic tested for each on any host, and `command`, the one way to make a child process (on Windows without a console window of its own, `design/windows.md`); `platform/paths.rs` the rules of a path's text on either kind of machine (`Style`: Unix's or Windows', from an `Os` or from the path's own form; its name, folder, separator, joining and showing it) and the home folder as `~` (`home_relative`), so the window reads a server's paths by that server's rules (`design/remote.md`).
- `app.rs` holds `Root`, the window: its connection, switching servers, the layout, and the hosts for the docked panel, sheets, dialogs and toasts; `app/connection.rs` a connection's fields and streams (`Connection`: its queue, `view` and `surface`, grid and size, and whether a line is still its own), one for the window (`Root::own`) and one for a machine's tab; `app/machine.rs` a machine's tab (`MachineTab`, `Root::shown`, what each stream end does (a refused one, status 4, is `Lost::Restarted`), `MachineTab::stream_boot` for the flag of `surface` and `events --host`, keys, steering the view, colours, closing a pane, a tab or an agent there and reading an agent's context: `Closable`, `MachineRun`, `still_on`, `publish_tab` for what `Store` knows of the shown tab); `app/machine/acts.rs` the machine's acts (marking, moving, switching accounts and reading its list, and the tab's gates for `--boot`: `sends_boot`, `boot_note`, `carried_boot` and `streamed_boot`); `app/toasts.rs` the toasts' stack and what a toast's button does; `app/status.rs` the status bar (`app/status/key.rs`: `StatusKey`); `app/part.rs` `Part` (a cached view that asks `Root` to draw its element: `Drawn`, `cached`), `Parts` (four parts: the sidebar, the rail, the tab strip and the status bar; two orbit layers and their spots; `Told`, what `Root` last told each; the Store's and the chips' counts), `told` and `minded` (the two decisions), `Root::tell_parts`, run on every notification of `Root`, the backstop `mind_the_parts`, `follow_parts`, `chips_moved` and the beat (`keep_beating`, `follow_frames`) that asks the sidebar's orbit layer for a frame on the display's frames; `app/revised.rs` `Revised`, a value that counts every change (`Root::session`); `app/surface.rs` the window's own `surface` stream: opening it once the view shows a tab, reopening it, and waiting while the view shows none or a machine's tab shows; `app/keeper.rs` `Keeper`, the global that keeps `layout.json` (what it holds, when it writes, its toasts) and the `Root` methods that connect it to the window; `app/placement.rs` the window's `Placed` place and state, where the window opens and what a move or resize keeps (it imports nothing from the keeper); `app/widths.rs` the widths restored at launch, the fit of the sidebar and the dock to the window's width (`fit`, `Root::fit`) and the hook that reports a drag; `app/frame.rs` the frame the window draws itself on Linux (`Frame`, read from gpui's `Decorations`: the margin, corners, line and shadow, the resize grips, the title row that moves and zooms the window, and the minimise, maximise and close buttons), the window options that ask for it, and fitting the window to a window manager's frame (`fit_to_frame`, `min_size`); `app/frame/win32.rs` asks DWM for Windows 11's acrylic backdrop (`no_win32.rs` elsewhere); `app/dock.rs` the docked panel's card, the cached view it draws the panels `PanelKind::cached` lists in (`dock_view`), the settle that redraws a dock once its slide-in ends (`settle_dock`), and placing it over the terminal for a file's full screen (`app/dock/key.rs`: `DockKey`, the shown dock, Store notifications and the session's revision, told from `observe_self` like the parts' keys). `terminal.rs` (keys, ⌘ shortcuts, the surface, `keep_the_keys` and `focus_lost`, which give the keys to the terminal or the dock card when nothing holds them; `terminal/` holds `machine_menu` (a machine's pane menu: moving, marking and switching accounts there too), `machine_card` (what the terminal's place shows while a machine's workspace is picked, `Root::machine_tab_area`, and the one place told of a change, `machine_place_changed`), `mouse`, `selection`, `pane_menu`, `pane_accounts` (the account chips in a card's bottom padding and the menu of accounts they and `Switch account` open), `pane_notes` (the band of a held message and `↓ n more`), `escape` (`Root::escape`: the scene read from the window and the step taken, a docked panel's `tops` asked and its `close_top` called), `steps` (the keys that go to the next workspace, project or agent that needs you, and the mark), `pane_drag` (moving a pane by its handle or the right button, on a machine's tab too: pressing, dragging, dropping with `move-pane`, the handle and the layer the ghost flies in), `input`, `pasting` (⌘V of an image and files dropped on a pane, through `paste-file`), `links` (the path or web address under the pointer: `link --json` asked beside the queue for a path, an address found in the row itself, the underline, the hand, a click that opens a path in the Files panel and an address in the Browser; `Pointer` holds it with the wheel's remainder) `popup` (a plugin's popup: follows `surface --popup` while the app feed names one, draws it over the terminal, types into it with `input --popup`) and `no_tab` (the terminal's place while the view shows no tab: "No tab open" with its buttons, or the welcome when there is no project)), `sidebar.rs` (the sidebar's frame and `card`, the floating card the list and the rail each sit in; `sidebar/` holds `key` (`SidebarKey`, what the sidebar draws as a plain value, and `Root::sidebar_key`), `rail` with `rail/key` and `rail/gear` (the card of panel icons left of the sidebar, a cached part, `RailKey`, and the gear: the sheets behind it and its menu), `look` (the `sidebar`, `tabs`, `agents_section`, `account_mark` and `account_switcher` settings it follows), `arrange` (the cards of each arrangement), `rows`, `workspace` (workspace and agent rows), `tab_rows`, `agents` (the agents section), `machine_rows` (a machine's workspaces, tabs and projects, and their folds), `machine_agents` (a machine's rows in the agents section), `machine_acts` (its menus, rename and close), `menus`, `actions`, `opening` (opening a project, importing a VS Code workspace), `forms` with `forms/style` and `forms/checked` (the close-agent check, here or on a machine's tab: `AgentCloseThere`), `order` for drag ordering, and `folds` for folds shown before the session confirms them), `tabs.rs` (the tab bar, its strip a cached part; `tabs/key.rs`: `TabsKey`; `tabs/acts.rs`: its actions and menu; `tabs/strip.rs` scrolls it when the tabs do not fit and keeps the active one in view, and its watcher, which spans the tab slot, tells the names how wide they may be: `name_widest`) and `context_menu.rs` hold the rest of `Root`'s methods. `escape.rs` is Escape's order as plain data (`Scene`, `Focus`, `Top`, `Step`): from what is open and where the focus is, the one step a press takes (`design/terminal.md`). `keymap.rs` is the table of ⌘ shortcuts (commands, keys, labels for tooltips and help) and how a keystroke reads as one on each platform (`chord`: Ctrl+Shift on Linux), plain data with no window; `dialogs/shortcuts.rs` draws it as the ⌘/ dialog.
- `cli.rs` is everything that talks to cornercase (`cli/features.rs`: `Feature` and `Has`, what a server's release has; `cli/update.rs` reads `update`'s answer (`update_answer`, the line of `update --json` by its status) and chooses its command by the releases (`UPDATE_JSON_SINCE`, `UpdateGate`, `update_command`); `cli/holds.rs`'s `Ended` carries the last line a hold printed; `with_boot` writes `--boot` once; `StreamEnd` and `refused_boot` tell status 4 by the status; a machine's queue sends its run's boot): one-shot commands (`run`, or `run_on` a given `Target`, as a machine's; `run_partial` and `run_partial_on` also take exit 3 as an answer and keep exit 4 as a stale boot), long-running streams (`stream_on`, a `Stream` stops its process when dropped) and an ordered `Queue` for input (`Queue::new` on the window's target, `Queue::on` on a machine's, `Queue::nowhere` while it is down), which tells the window when a command moves its view (`moves_view`). The window never speaks the socket protocol itself. `Target` says where commands run (`connect` changes it); `cli/remote.rs` holds the `--remote`/`--remote-command` flags a `Target::Remote` adds and the reconnect back-off (`design/remote.md`); `cli/locate.rs` finds the binary (the `PATH`, the login shell's, the installers' folders) and checks its version, with `cli/locate/system.rs` running the processes; `cli/typist.rs` the long-running `input --follow --json -` the queue types through; `cli/requests.rs` the long-running `requests --follow -` its commands go through, and how a line and its answer read; `cli/kept.rs` the keeper of children that must be asked to end (`SIGTERM` on Unix, `Child::kill` on Windows, where each is held by a Job object (`cli/kept/win32.rs`, `no_win32.rs` elsewhere) so that a window that dies takes it along), which either lets go of what the child started (`End::LetGo`) or takes it along (`End::TakeAlong`); `cli/window_processes.rs` the one `plugin windows --json` the window keeps while it is on this computer (`design/plugins.md`); `cli/holds.rs` the `tunnel hold` children, one per tunnel, kept with `TakeAlong`: `PORT`, `PORT --local N` and `--reverse LOCAL`, `Holding` being the one way to start any of them (`design/browser.md`); `cli/partial.rs` `run_partial`, which takes exit 3 (a whole answer some of whose sources failed) as success, `answer_on` and `answer_at`, which keep a whole command's output as an `Answer` and read it as a `Said` (an update's or restart's outcome, shown on a page), where only exit 0 is `Done` and exit 3 is a failure, and `run_printed`, which keeps what a command printed before it failed (`plugin run`'s outcome). `cli/older.rs` reads a refusal by an older cornercase there (status 5 and its `--json` line), what a start answers and what follows it, the `update` words. `reason` reads a failed command's error as a sentence, without `cornercase config: error:` around it, for errors a sheet shows in place.
- `model.rs` holds the serde types of `view` (the session: projects, workspaces, tabs, agents) and the readings of it that the sidebar and Search share (`Session::sections`, the sidebar's order; `PaneInfo::agent_title`; `Session::path_style`, the rules of the server's paths; `Session::release`, the release of the server that sent it); `model/pull_request.rs` a workspace's pull request (its words on GitHub or GitLab, its summary and what to tell an agent, every check once `pull-request --json` gave them, its stack's layers and what blocks them, the workspace that holds a layer); `model/agent_settings.rs` an agent's modes and the default agent as `config --json` lists them, here and on a machine, and which mode shows, rests and is sent, by the release of the server that starts it; `model/reopen.rs` what one Reopen of a workspace brings back (`Workspace::reopen`, `Reopen`: its closed agents first, else its run's exited roles; the one rule the run's page and the sidebar's menu ask); `model/shared.rs` the files a workspace shares with others (how many, what its row and a file's tooltip say); `model/tree.rs` the walk that makes agents a tree, for any key; `model/machines/seen/agents.rs` the agents section's rows, ours and each machine's, in order, and a fold aimed at a pane there (`PaneAim`, `sighted_pane`); `model/machines/seen.rs` holds the six releases that gate `--boot` (`PANE_BOOT_SINCE`, `FOLD_BOOT_SINCE`, `HAND_OFF_BOOT_SINCE`, `ACTS_BOOT_SINCE`, `READS_BOOT_SINCE` and `READS_BOOT_THERE_SINCE`, the last two compared through `ReadReleases`) and what a `view` line says of each machine (`MachineSeen`, `ThereProject`, `ThereWorkspace`, `ThereId`, whose ids are another server's; `Aim` and the commands on a workspace there; `MachinePlace`, a picked place, and how it follows a snapshot; and the `Session` readings that place rows); `model/machines.rs` what `machines --json` and `status --app` say of the machines (`Machine`, its `LinkState` and `MachineIcon`, its state in words, `in_app`, the one parser of the list), for whatever shows them; `model/details.rs` what a tab's row and the keys read from the session (`Tab::memory`, `memory_size`, `Session::project_order`, `Session::agent_needing_you`); `model/agent_context.rs` what `context --json` answers (`ContextAnswer`, its `Breakdown`, a category's place by its kind, the tools that took the most); `model/accounts.rs` what `account list --json` answers, shared with the Accounts sheet, the defaults of `status --app` (`Selection`) and their readings for a pane (its mark, its switchers, each switch's command and what its answer says); `model/accounts/pick.rs` what a form that starts an agent can offer (`AgentChoices`), the state of one choice (`Picking`) and what it sends (`AccountPick`, `account_args`); `model/surface.rs` those of `surface` (frames, patches, the split tree), `model/grid.rs` the grid they build up, and `model/link.rs` what `link --json` answers (`Link`), the link under the pointer (`Hovered`, its `Span` and its `Target`, a file or a web address), the web addresses of a row (`url_in`, `web_hyperlink`) and the text of a row's cells. `model/owner.rs` is `Owner` (a Browser tab's owner: a workspace of this server, a linked machine's `WorkspaceThere` from `model/machines/seen.rs`, or nobody); `PaneThere`, beside `PaneAim` in `model/machines/seen/agents.rs`, is a machine's agent as a hand-off aims at it, and `MachineOpen` in `model/tunnels.rs` says whether a machine's port can open.
- `render.rs` draws each pane on its card, its cells with `Metrics` measured from the font; `render/popup.rs` sizes and paints the popup's card; `render/layout.rs` places the cards from the surface's split tree (`Layout`), tells how many cells fit once padding and gaps are taken out, and maps the pointer back to a pane's cells or to the gap of a split; `render/moving.rs` is a pane move as plain data over time (`Moves`: the press, where it would land, the ghost's flight, the cards sliding to their new places, the landing glow), tested without a window, and `render/ghost.rs` paints what it decides (the ghost, the drop zone, its caption and the glow). `keys.rs` encodes gpui keystrokes for `cornercase input`. `theme.rs` reads the theme in use (`design/theme.md`): the terminal palette (also given to cornercase with `start-server --theme` and `theme -`), the chrome colours (`theme::colours`), the corner radii (`theme::radius`), the terminal's opacity, `Painter` (a pane's colours over the theme's terminal) and `shade`; `theme/` holds `colour` (hex, mixing, luminance), `tokens` (the token list and its macro, opacity limits, shape), `file` (a theme file that keeps keys it does not know), `resolve` (`extends`, derived colours, warnings), `presets` (`assets/themes/`), `contrast` (ratios and Fix), `current` (the static) and `fonts`; `appearance.rs` is the global entity that owns the choice and the user's themes, with `appearance/` for the choice file, files, edits, watching and sharing, and `app/looks.rs` gives `Root` the settled palette, the toasts of problems, the window's light or dark and Peek's layer; `over_ground` (a colour that looks over the ground as it would over the desktop), the ground per platform (`ground`), and `FONT_FAMILY` and `UI_FAMILY`, which `theme/fonts.rs` resolves at start to fonts installed on Linux.
- `tour.rs` holds `Guide`, one per `Store` (a global, drawn by `Root` over everything): it follows the app feed's `tour`, opens what a step shows and reports the steps the window completes; `tour/model.rs` the tours' serde types and how steps map to panels, `tour/card.rs` the card, `tour/catalogue.rs` the Help dialog: the docs and bug links, the tours, resetting their progress.
- `notify.rs` follows `cornercase events` (`notify/feed.rs` parses them; `notify/telling.rs` is what a notice or a `message` event becomes: toast, button, headline, what is dropped; `until_seen` holds a merged run's toast while the window is behind): agent notices as toasts with a "Go" button, desktop notifications, sounds, OSC 52 copies, the bell and the window title. `notify/update.rs` also draws both update dialogs' Copy command (`copy_command`) and the words of each outcome, and `notify/update/outcome.rs` reads an install's outcome from `update_answer`; `Notifier::tell` holds the update toasts in `later` until the window is active; `notify/alert.rs` builds the notification and the sound per platform (the app's own notification through `notify/center.rs` from the bundle, else `osascript`, and `afplay`, `notify-send` and `paplay` with the freedesktop sounds, or a Windows toast and `PlaySound` with Windows' own sounds), and runs them; `notify/alert/win32.rs` holds the Windows calls (`no_win32.rs` their stubs elsewhere). `notify/center.rs` is macOS's own notifications: whether this run posts them (`available`, the main bundle's identifier), what a refusal says, the notices posted (`Posted`, kept in `Store::posted`) and what a click on one does (`listen`, from `main.rs`, through `Store::go`); `notify/center/cocoa.rs` holds the Objective-C (authorization, posting, the delegate), `no_cocoa.rs` its stubs elsewhere. `notify/update.rs` offers cornercase's updates and restarts (`Chip`, and `installed`, which also counts the `cornercase` on disk), `UpdateToast` and `Updating` (a toast that opens an update dialog), and draws the release notes both update dialogs show (`release_notes`, with `ParsedNotes`, which parses them only when they change).
- `self_update.rs` is the window's own update: `Updater`, a global entity made in `main.rs` that asks GitHub for the latest release at start and every hour, keeps what it found, installs it and prepares the relaunch (`self_update/`: `release` reads GitHub's answer, compares versions, picks the notes, the asset and checks a checksum, `fetch` is the only file that uses `ureq`, `install` decides how this copy updates and does it, `arrival` is the note a restart leaves for the next start (`relaunch.json`), `bundle` is macOS's part of it (the app around the program, the DMG, the signature check, the swap and the relaunch through `open`), `relaunch` is what starts the new program, `dialog` the dialog). `main.rs` also answers `--version`. `platform/probe.rs` runs a program for a few lines of its output within a time limit (the `cornercase --version` check and the downloaded program's).
- `desktop.rs` is what the desktop the window runs on offers: its name for this machine (`machine`, `here`: "this Mac", "this computer" or "this PC"), its file manager's name and menu labels, opening a path with its default app (`open`) and showing it in the file manager (`reveal`) through `open`, `xdg-open`, the freedesktop `FileManager1` D-Bus call or Windows' shell (the Win32 calls in `desktop/win32.rs`, which also names the app for the taskbar, with stubs in `desktop/no_win32.rs` elsewhere), what counts as a program that opening would run (`runs_when_opened`), running such a tool with a deadline and its last words as the error (`run`), private temporary folders and files (`Scratch`, which goes when dropped), finding tools (`Lookup`, through `PATHEXT` on Windows), the XDG data folders and the Windows folder, and the primary selection on Linux (`Board`, where a finished selection goes on each platform, `read_primary`, `write_primary`).

## Store

`terminal/missing.rs` draws the page for a missing or unusable cornercase and lets the user retry or choose a binary, kept in `settings.json` as `cornercase_bin`, and offers the update of one too old. `terminal/older.rs` draws the pages for an older cornercase on another machine and for its server still on the old release, with their update and restart.

`store.rs` holds `Store` (`server_has` asks `cli::Feature`), a global gpui entity (`Store::get(cx)`) every part of the window shares: `session` (the view's snapshot), `view_id`, `app` (what `status --app --follow` prints), `accounts` (what `account list --json` said last, asked again when `status --app`'s `accounts_revision` or the view's config revision moves, `accounts_changes` counting what changed), `selection` (the defaults in `app`), `account_settings` (`account_mark`, `account_picker` and the default agent of `config --json`, handed over by `sidebar/look.rs`; `account_choices` is what forms offer), `tunnels` (this window's holds, keyed by where they go: on the server it shows when the window is on another server, or on the machines it links when it is on this computer, `design/machines.md`), `there` (what it knows of machines: the home folders it asked, this computer's cornercase release as `read_cli_release` reads it, and `tab`, the shown machine tab as `Root::publish_tab` tells it: `TabThere`), `machines` (its `machines`, parsed there once, which every change's `--json` answer replaces through `take_machines`, `design/machines.md`), the open `dock`, `sheet` and `dialog`, the `toasts`, `posted` (the notifications this window posted on macOS, so a click on one finds its action, `design/notifications.md`), `openers` (the apps of this machine that open a path, found once off the main thread, `files/open.rs`), the window's own settings (`prefs`: `prefs.rs` reads and writes them, `Store::set_pref` changes one, `toggle_long_lines` the one the viewer's and the diff's button share), whether a file has the terminal's area (`file_full_screen`, `shows_file_full_screen`, `design/files-panel.md`), what the plugins offer the window (`plugins`, `panels/plugins/offers.rs`), and the `jobs` that run (`store/jobs.rs`: `Busy`, a job's key and what it says (`reopening` and `reopen_key`, the one Reopen job, started through `Store::reopen_agents` and `Store::run_aside_once`), and `Jobs`, the running ones, their summary for the status bar and the workspaces whose worktree is being removed, here or on a machine), and `machine_place`, a machine's workspace picked in the sidebar; `store/machines_there.rs` holds what acts on a machine: `machine_target` (whether and how it can be reached from this window), `reach_machine`, `act_on_machine` with `MachineAct`, `worktree_went_there` (a machine's worktree removed, its row kept until the snapshot drops it), `pick_machine_place` and `leave_machine_place`, a notice's `go_there`, its home, and `cli_release`, which a machine's fold and a machine tab's `--boot` gates both read (`design/machines.md`).

- `store/toasts.rs` holds `Toast`, `ToastKind`, `Shown` (a toast's seconds counted while it is drawn), `push_toast` and who makes way past five, and `Store::toast_with`, `Store::toasts_drawn` (what the stack drew, which starts the sweeps) and the sweeps.
- `Store::act(args)` runs a command **with `--view ID`** (focus, new-tab, split, start, send, keys, read, wait, mark, rename, open, new-workspace, go…) through the ordered queue; `Store::cmd(args)` does the same without the view. Errors become toasts.
- `store::run(cx, args, |this, Result<String>, cx| …)` and `store::run_json(…)` run a command off the main thread and give its output back to the entity.
- `store::run_on(cx, Target, args, …)` and `store::follow_on(cx, &Target, args, …)` run or follow a command on a given target, a machine's; `store::follow` and `follow_until` are the latter on the window's own.
- `Store::shown_owner()` (`store/browse.rs`) is the place the Browser panel follows (`model/owner.rs`: `Owner::{Here, There, Nobody}`, a machine's workspace as `WorkspaceThere` from `model/machines/seen.rs`); the same file holds `open_in_browser` and `open_tunnel` (`design/machines.md`, "A machine's page in the Browser panel").
- `store/update_there.rs` is the update of a machine's cornercase: the window's older page shares its question, its job (key and label) and `run_update`; the offer, the words of each answer (`updated_words` for the toast, `update_page` for the older page) and the probe afterwards serve the four strips, the machine card and the tunnels menu.
- `Store::machine_read()` (`store/machine_read.rs`) says what Changes and Files read while a machine's workspace is picked: its `Aim`, its path and how it is reached (`Reach`, `design/remote.md`; the same file holds the kept older refusal, its probe and a machine's home, `design/machines.md`); `Store::agent_reach` says whether a machine's agent can still be read (`TabThere`, `AgentReach`, published by `Root::publish_tab`).
- `store::run_busy(cx, Busy, args, …)` does what `store::run` does while a job says it runs, `store::run_partial_busy` what `store::run_partial` does, and `store::run_partial_on_busy` the same on a given target; `Store::act_busy(Busy, args)` is `act` with a job, through the queue; `Store::run_aside(Busy, args)` runs a command beside the queue as a job and toasts its error, and `Store::run_aside_once(Busy, args)` does the same but is dropped while a job of that key runs; `Store::start_here(StartHere)` runs a `start --placed` of the shown dialog that way, keeps that dialog on `dialogs::dialog_starts` until it answers and then closes it and the sheet, as `Store::start_there` does for a machine; `Store::switch_account(Switch)` runs a pane's `account restart` that way and toasts what it answered; `Store::run_then(Busy, work, then)` runs any work as a job whose answer comes back to the store, for what must outlive the dialog that asked (a worktree's removal), and `Store::busy(key)` and `Store::removing(workspace)` tell what runs; `store::run_local(cx, Busy, work)` runs work of this machine rather than a command (opening a file in an app) off the main thread as a job and toasts its error; `store::run_local_busy(cx, Busy, work, then)` is that work as a job whose result comes back to `then` (the window's own update).
- `store::queued(cx, args, |Result<String>, cx| …)` runs a command through the ordered queue and gives its answer back on the main thread; `dialogs::Question::on_reply` does the same for a confirmation; `store::busy_reply(cx, Busy, then)` starts a job and gives the reply for any queue's `cmd_then`, and `store::reply(cx, then)` does the same without a job.
- `store::follow(cx, args, |this, line, cx| …) -> Option<Stream>` follows a streaming command, one line per change. The entity keeps the `Stream`; dropping it stops the process.
- `Store::go(action)` does what a toast's button does (`ToastGo`), for the toast and for a click on its notification; a toast that opens an update dialog leaves `Updating` in `Store::asks` (with `refocus`) for `Root::take_asks`.
- `Store::copy_from(path, place)` copies a path with its place named; `toast(text, ToastKind, cx)`, `ask_agent(workspace, text, label)` (pastes the text into the workspace's agent, focuses it and gives it the keys, or copies it when there is none), `ask_about(workspace, reference)` (the same with a `path:a-b ` reference), `open_in_editor(workspace, path, line)` (a new tab running the editor there), `show_in_terminal(kind, id)` (focuses a pane, workspace or project and gives the keys to the terminal, which `Root` does on seeing `keys_to_terminal`), `copy_elsewhere(path)` (a path on another machine: copied, with a toast that says where it is), `give_keys_back()` (the same without moving the focus elsewhere), `open(kind)`, `toggle_dock(kind)`, `open_on_project(kind, project)` (a sheet that shows that project, which takes it with `take_project_sheet`), `read_run_file(path)` (the Files panel shows a run's file and goes back to the Flow panel), `open_in_browser(url, owner)` (`store/browse.rs`, with `shown_owner`, `open_tunnel` and `toast_go`, kept out of `store/tunnels.rs`; `MachineOpen` is in `model/tunnels.rs`) (the Browser panel shows it, through `browse`), `run_started(workspace)` (the Flows panel selects the run started there, now or once it is built), `close_sheet()`, `show_dialog(AnyView)`, `close_dialog()`, and `connect(Target)`, which leaves the target in `switch_to` for the window to switch to.

## Panels
